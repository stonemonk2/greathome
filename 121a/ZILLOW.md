### One accuracy note

The floor is **12mm AC5 laminate** (Republic Floor, North Forest), not luxury
vinyl plank — the invoice calls it LVP but the spec says laminate. AC5 is the
top abrasion rating, so this is not a downgrade to admit; it is a better number
than most renovated rentals can quote. Just do not print "luxury vinyl plank."

## Craigslist version

Craigslist rewards plain text and a clear title. Post under
**housing → apts/housing for rent**, roughly $5 in the Bay Area.

**Title:** `Renovated 1BR + Office in Point Richmond — walk to everything — $2600`

Use the same body as above. Craigslist allows the link, so put
`https://www.greathome.us/121a` near the top as well as the bottom, and upload
the best 12–16 photos directly (Craigslist users largely do not click through).

**Repost every 48 hours** — Craigslist ranks by recency and a listing falls off
the first page fast.

Label every virtually staged image **"Virtually staged"** in its caption.
Zillow's listing policy requires the disclosure and prospects expect staging —
what they resent is discovering it.

## After you relist

Give it **14 days** before touching the price. You have never actually tested
$2,600 with real photos, so the silence to date is not market data.

If inquiries are still thin after two weeks, drop to **$2,495** — not $2,550.
$2,500 is the most common max-price filter renters set, and at $2,600 you are
invisible to everyone using it. At $2,495 you become the best unit at the top of
a much larger pool.
